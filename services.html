Classic Set

Enhance your natural beauty with our Classic Set. Each extension is applied one-to-one on your natural lashes, creating a soft, timeless look that adds length and definition without feeling heavy. Perfect for everyday wear or first-time lash clients.

Hybrid Set

The best of both worlds — Hybrid combines the natural elegance of Classic with the fluffy fullness of Volume. A mix of single lashes and light fans are applied for a textured, wispy look that's versatile and eye-catching. Perfect if you want something fuller than Classic but softer than Volume.

KSh 2,500

Volume Set

Turn up the drama with our Volume Set. Multiple lightweight lash extensions are fanned and applied to each natural lash, creating a full, fluffy, and glamorous look. Perfect for special occasions or anyone who loves bold, eye-catching lashes that still feel soft and weightless.

KSh 2,000

Lash Fill

Keep your lashes looking fresh and flawless with regular refills. During a refill, grown-out extensions are safely removed and new ones are applied to maintain your full set. Recommended every 2–3 weeks to keep your lashes looking their best.
